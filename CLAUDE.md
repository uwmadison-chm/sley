Sley is a set of standards and tools for organizing and processing human subjects data -- self-report, experience sampling (EMA), passive sensing, behavioral data, and the like.

The project is primarily being developed by Nate Vack at the Center for Healthy Minds at UW-Madison; when I say "me," that's generally who I am talking about. I'm using primarily Claude Code to assist in development, when I say "you" or that's generally who I am talking about, or "we" when working together as a team. We'll primary use "researchers" or "they" to refer to people who use this program.

As of writing, this project is in the design phase -- there's no code yet. The plan is to start small, with immediately useful tools.
